## 4. The door-to-door pitch

You are standing in the shop. The owner is busy. You have about twenty seconds
before they decide whether to listen.

**Do not open with WebSetu. Open with them.**

> "Namaskar. Main Aetherdev se hoon, Pune se. Ek minute hai?
>
> Aapka shop Google pe search kiya maine — [say what you actually found: listing
> hai par photo nahi / kuch nahi mila / number purana hai].
>
> Main website banata hoon local businesses ke liye. Aapko kuch dena nahi hai
> abhi — main yahin baith ke, abhi, aapki website bana ke dikhata hoon. Pandrah
> minute. Pasand aayi to rakhiye, nahi aayi to delete kar dete hain."

**Then open the laptop and actually do it.** The demo IS the pitch. Do not
describe the product — build their site in front of them, with their name, their
photos, their services.

**At the end:**

> "Ye aapki website hai. Teen din free chalegi. Link WhatsApp pe bhej deta hoon —
> aap ghar ja ke dekh lijiye, ghar walon ko dikha dijiye. Teen din baad agar
> rakhna ho to ₹999 mahina, warna kuch nahi."

**What to carry:** a charged laptop, a phone hotspot, and 3–4 photos already on
the laptop for a generic shop in case theirs has none.

## 5. Phone script

For the ones you cannot walk into.

> "Namaskar, [name] ji? Main [your name], Aetherdev Solutions, Pune se.
>
> Main local businesses ki website banata hoon. Aapko abhi kuch nahi bechna hai —
> ek sawaal poochhna tha. Aapka business online kahin hai? Website ya Google pe?"

Then **shut up and listen.** Whatever they say, the next line is the same:

> "Theek hai. Main aapko ek link bhejta hoon WhatsApp pe — ek fruit shop ki
> website hai jo humne banayi. Do minute dekh lijiye. Agar aapko lage ki aapke
> business ke liye kaam ki cheez hai to reply kar dijiye, main bana dunga. Teen
> din free hai."

Send the link within one minute of hanging up, while they still remember.

**If they ask the price on the call:** answer it straight. "₹999 mahina.
Saal bhar ka lo to do mahine free. Teen din free trial hai, pehle chala ke dekh
lijiye." Never say "let me tell you later" — it reads as expensive.

## 6. Answers to what they will actually ask

**"Mera business chhota hai, website ki kya zarurat?"**
> "Chhota business hi sabse zyada zarurat rakhta hai. Bade brand ko log naam se
> dhoondhte hain. Aapko log kaam se dhoondhte hain — 'fruit shop near me'. Wahan
> dikhne ke liye kuch to online hona chahiye."

**"Mera to WhatsApp business hai, kaafi hai."**
> "WhatsApp bilkul rakhiye — hum usko hataate nahi. Par WhatsApp pe woh log
> message karte hain jinke paas aapka number pehle se hai. Naya customer aapka
> number kahan se laayega?"

**"₹999 mahina zyada hai."**
> "Ek baar banwane ka kaam mehnga padta hai, aur baad mein har change ka alag
> paisa. Yahan mahine ka ₹999, aur badalna aap khud kar sakte ho. Aur teen din
> free hai — paisa tab dena jab aapko lage kaam ki hai."

> **Apna number daal sakte ho.** Tum Pune mein agency chalate ho — tumhe pata hai
> yahan ek one-time website ka kya rate chalta hai. Wo asli figure is line mein
> daal do, mere anumaan se behtar hai. Bas wahi bolna jo tum sach mein jaante ho.

**"Mujhe computer nahi aata."**
> "Isliye main aa raha hoon. Pehli website main banaunga aapke saamne. Baad mein
> badalna ho to phone karo, ya khud kar lo — utna hi aasan hai jitna WhatsApp pe
> photo bhejna."

**"Sochkar batata hoon."**
> "Bilkul. Main link bhej deta hoon, teen din free chalegi — aap chala ke dekhiye.
> Sochne se accha hai chala ke dekhna. Teen din baad main phone karunga."

Do not argue with this one. Send the link. Call on day three.

## 9. The coupon

`WEB20` — **20% off**, 100 uses, active right now.

Use it as a reason to act today, not as a permanent discount:

```
Is hafte join karne walon ke liye WEB20 — 20% off.
Starter ₹999 ➝ ₹799 pehle mahine.
```

Do not put the coupon on the website. Give it out in person, on calls, and in
one-to-one WhatsApp messages. A discount somebody was given feels different from
a discount everybody gets.

## What to track

You do not need a spreadsheet. A page in a notebook, four columns:

| Date | Who | What happened | Next |
|---|---|---|---|

Log every conversation. After 20, read the "What happened" column top to bottom.
The sentence that keeps appearing is your real marketing message — and it will
almost certainly not be one of the ones written above.

Replace this file with what you learn.
