# How to use WebSetu

Your business website, live today. This guide takes about 15 minutes to follow,
and you do not need to know anything about computers.

Keep three things ready before you start:

- Your business name, and the area you work in
- Your phone number and WhatsApp number
- 3–4 photos — your shop, your work, your team

That is all. Everything else, we fill in for you.

---

## Step 1 — Create your account

Go to **www.websetu.instantqr.tech** and press **Create Your Website**.

Enter your name, email and a password. That is the whole signup. No card, no
payment. Your **3-day free trial starts immediately**, and your website is live
during it — so people can see it while you decide.

> If you already have an account, press **Log in** instead.

---

## Step 2 — The setup wizard (7 steps)

After signup you land in the setup wizard. It asks you seven short things. Nothing
here is permanent — you can change every one of them later.

**1. Business** — your business name, what kind of business it is (choose from the
list — pick the closest one, this decides how Google understands you), and your
city.

**2. Branding** — pick a colour palette. There are more than twenty, arranged by
type of business: green for organic and farm, terracotta for pottery and interiors, navy
for corporate, maroon for jewellery. Pick the one that feels like your shop. You
can change it any time.

**3. Services** — what you actually sell or do. Type three or four. The wizard
suggests common ones for your category — a plumber gets "leak repair", "bathroom
fitting"; a salon gets "haircut", "bridal makeup". Tap to add.

**4. Contact** — phone, WhatsApp number, address, and your working hours. Fill the
hours in properly: your website shows a live **"Open now"** or **"Closed"** badge
from them, and Google uses them too.

**5. Template** — pick a layout. All of them work on phones.

**6. Preview** — see your website before anyone else does.

**7. Publish** — press **Create My Website**. Your site goes live straight away.

> It can only go live if it has your **address and city** — Google and your
> customers both need them. Leave those blank and the site is created but stays
> private until you add them in Business Profile and press Publish.

**The AI writes your content here.** Your headline, your About paragraph, your
service descriptions — all drafted from what you typed. It is a first draft, not
the final word. Change anything you do not like.

---

## Step 3 — Your website is live

You now have a real website at:

```
www.websetu.instantqr.tech/s/your-business-name
```

**This is the link you send to customers.** Find it in your dashboard sidebar
under **"Your public link"** — there is a copy button next to it.

Do not send the `/preview/` link. That one only opens for you; anybody else gets
a login screen.

> **Want your own address like `yourshop.in`?** That is a paid add-on. Message us
> and we will arrange the domain and connect it for you.

---

## Step 4 — Make it yours

Open **Website Builder** in the sidebar. On the left is a list of sections; on
the right is a live preview that updates as you type.

### The sections you get

| Section | What it does |
|---|---|
| Hero banner | The big headline and photo at the top |
| Statistics | Years of experience, customers served, jobs done |
| About us | Your story, with a photo beside it |
| Services | What you offer, with prices if you want |
| Why choose us | What makes you different |
| Gallery | Photos of your work |
| Products | A catalogue with prices and photos |
| Testimonials | What your customers say about you |
| FAQs | The questions you answer on the phone every day |
| Payment | Your UPI ID and QR code — customers pay from the website |
| Blog | Articles, if you want them |
| Contact | Map, form, phone, WhatsApp |

Every section has a toggle. Switch off the ones you do not need. Drag the handle
on the left to reorder them.

### To edit a section

Press the **pencil** icon next to it. Change the text, upload a photo, press
**Save Draft**.

### Adding photos

Anywhere you see a **Photo** or **Upload** box, press it and choose a picture from
your phone or computer. Pictures are resized for you before they reach a
visitor, so the page stays fast on mobile data.

One limit: a single picture must be **under 4 MB**. Most phone photos are well
under that; a very large one is refused, and any phone's gallery app can shrink
it.

Good photos to use: your shopfront the way it looks from the road, your team, the
actual work you have done. Ten real photos beat one downloaded picture. Customers
can tell the difference immediately.

### Colours and fonts

Open **Theme & Branding** at the top. Choose a palette, or set each colour by
hand. Change the font, corner style and layout width. Watch the preview change
as you press.

> On the free trial and unpaid plans you get **5 design changes**. A paid plan is
> unlimited.

---

## Step 5 — Publish your changes

Editing does not change your live site until you say so.

- **Save Draft** — saves your work, visitors still see the old version
- **Publish** (the **Live ▾** button, top right) — pushes it to the real website

So you can take your time. Nothing goes public by accident.

---

## Step 6 — Take payments on your website

Open **Business Profile** and fill in your **UPI ID** (like `yourname@okaxis`), or
upload your payment **QR code** photo.

A payment block appears on your website. A customer can pay you from the page —
GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM all open directly. The money goes straight to your
account. WebSetu does not touch it.

---

## Step 7 — Connect Google

In **Business Profile**, paste your **Google Business Profile URL** — the listing
that shows up on Google Maps when somebody searches your business name.

This matters more than anything else on this page. When your website and your
Google listing say the same name, same address, same phone number, Google is
confident they are the same business, and shows you more often to people
searching nearby.

If you do not have a Google listing yet, create one free at **google.com/business**
and verify it. It takes an evening and a postcard.

---

## Step 8 — Watch what happens

**Overview** shows how your site is doing. **Analytics** shows how many people
visited, which pages, and which buttons they pressed.

You will also get a notification when somebody fills your enquiry form — in the
app, by email, and on WhatsApp or SMS if you have set your number.

## Common questions

**Do I need to know coding?**
No. Nothing in WebSetu requires it.

**Can I change things later?**
Yes, all of it, any time. Text, photos, colours, sections, order.

**What happens after my trial ends?**
Your website and everything in it stays saved. It goes offline until you pick a
plan, and comes straight back when you do. Nothing is deleted.

**Will my website work on phones?**
Yes. Most of your customers will see it on a phone, so it is built for that
first.

**Can I use my own domain name?**
Yes — it is a paid add-on. Ask us and we will set it up.

**Who writes my content?**
The AI writes the first draft from your details. You edit whatever you want to
change. Most people change a line or two and leave the rest.

**How do I share my website?**
Copy **"Your public link"** from your dashboard sidebar and send it on WhatsApp.
It shows a proper preview card with your business name and photo.
