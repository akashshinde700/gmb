# Video scripts

Six videos. All shot on a phone, vertical, 9:16. None need editing beyond
trimming the ends. Total filming time if you do them back to back: about an hour.

**Rules that matter more than the script:**

- Vertical. Always. This is going on WhatsApp status and Instagram reels.
- Speak Marathi or Hindi if that is how you would actually say it. The written
  scripts below are Hinglish because that is how people in Pune and Satara talk.
  Do not translate them into formal Hindi — it will sound like an advertisement.
- Show the screen. Every second of a real website being built is worth ten
  seconds of you explaining it.
- Subtitles on. Most people watch with the sound off.
- Bad lighting near a window beats good lighting in a studio, because it looks
  real.

## Video 1 — "15 minutes" (the main one)

**Length:** 45–60 seconds
**Where:** WhatsApp status, Instagram reel, YouTube short
**Goal:** prove the claim, not describe it

This is the only video you actually need. Film it first.

| Time | Shot | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Your face, phone held up | "Aapka business hai, par online kuch nahi hai? Chaliye, abhi banate hain. Time dekh lijiye." |
| 0:05 | Screen recording — signup form | "Naam, email, password. Bas." |
| 0:10 | Screen — wizard step 1 | "Business ka naam. Kya kaam karte ho. Kaunsa sheher." |
| 0:18 | Screen — palette picker, tapping colours | "Rang chun lijiye." |
| 0:25 | Screen — services being added | "Kya kya karte ho — teen chaar likh dijiye." |
| 0:32 | Screen — phone, WhatsApp, hours | "Number, WhatsApp, timing." |
| 0:38 | Screen — the finished site scrolling | "Aur ye ban gaya. Content AI ne likha hai." |
| 0:48 | Your face, showing the phone with the site | "Pandrah minute. Link WhatsApp pe bhej sakte ho. Teen din free — card ki zarurat nahi." |
| 0:55 | Text on screen: `www.websetu.instantqr.tech` | — |

**Filming note:** actually do it in one take with a real business. A stopwatch on
screen makes the claim unarguable. If it takes 22 minutes, say 22 minutes — an
honest number is more convincing than a round one.

## Video 2 — "Google pe kaise dikhoge"

**Length:** 40 seconds
**Goal:** the reason a shop owner should care, in one idea

| Time | Shot | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Phone, typing into Google: "electrician near me" | "Aapke area mein kisi ko electrician chahiye. Wo aapka naam nahi likhta — usko naam pata hi nahi." |
| 0:08 | The Google results, three businesses with maps | "Ye teen aate hain. Aap inme kyun nahi?" |
| 0:15 | Screen — WebSetu Business Profile tab, Google listing field | "Website aur Google listing — dono pe ek hi naam, ek hi address, ek hi number." |
| 0:24 | Screen — the tenant site with map and hours | "Timing, map, direction — sab website pe. Google isko padhta hai." |
| 0:34 | Your face | "Ye free hai. Karna sirf ye hai ki sahi jagah likha ho." |

**Why this one works:** it does not sell a website. It sells being found. That is
what the shop owner actually wants.

**Goal:** one specific, painful, recognisable problem

| 0:06 | The message sitting there — just digits, nothing else | "Kisne save kiya? Kisi ne nahi." |

| 0:26 | Your face | "Link ek hi hai. Farak ye hai." |

**Filming note:** film the real thing. Send both messages to your own number and
screen-record it. Do not mock it up.

## Video 4 — "Customer ka reaction" (the one that sells)

**Length:** 30–45 seconds
**Goal:** proof from somebody who is not you

You cannot film this until you have a happy customer. **Film it the same day you
make one.**

**Format:** no script. Ask three questions and shut up.

1. "Pehle online kya tha aapka?"
2. "Kitna time laga?"
3. "Ab kya farak pada?"

Cut to the shop owner's face and their website side by side. Do not coach the
answers. A slightly awkward real answer beats a polished fake one, and everyone
watching can tell which is which.

> Right now the honest candidate is **Prasad Fruit Sales** — a real published site
> that has already received an enquiry through the form. That enquiry is the
> single most valuable asset in your marketing. Go and film it.

## Video 6 — "Kitna kharcha" (the objection)

**Length:** 35 seconds
**Goal:** answer the price question before they ask it

| Time | Shot | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Your face | "Sabse pehla sawaal — kitna lagega." |
| 0:06 | Text on screen: `₹999 / month` | "Website ka ₹999 mahina. Saal bhar loge to do mahine free." |
| 0:14 | Your face | "Ek baar banwane ka kaam mehnga padta hai — aur uske baad har chhote change ka phir paisa." |
| 0:22 | Screen — editing a section, pressing Publish | "Yahan aap khud badal sakte ho. Jitni baar chaho." |
| 0:30 | Your face | "Aur teen din free hai. Pehle chala ke dekho, phir decide karo." |

**Careful:** do not name competitors by name in the video. "Ek baar ka website
banwane wale" is enough and cannot get you into an argument.
