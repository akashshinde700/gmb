# WebSetu — marketing kit

Everything in this folder is written to be used, not admired. Copy a block, paste
it, send it. Nothing here needs a designer or an agency to sit in the middle.

| File | What it's for | Who reads it |
|---|---|---|

## The one thing to get right

WebSetu's customers are local business owners in Pune and Satara. They do not
read English marketing copy. They do not browse SaaS comparison sites. They
forward WhatsApp messages, they ask a friend, and they trust somebody who
showed up in person.

So every piece of content here is built for one of three moments:

1. **A forward** — somebody sends your message to a shop owner they know.
2. **A demo** — you are standing in the shop with a laptop.
3. **A search** — a business owner types "website banane wale Pune" into Google.

If a piece of content does not serve one of those three, it does not belong here.

> Encrypt certificate covers `www.websetu.instantqr.tech` only — so a browser

> the site. On a flyer that is unrecoverable: nobody types a URL twice.

## Things NOT to claim

Be careful here. Half of trust in this market is not overpromising.

- **Do not promise leads or enquiry numbers.** The lead feature works, but you
  have deliberately hidden it from the landing page, and you have no numbers to
  back a claim yet. Sell the website. Let leads be a surprise.
- **Do not say "Marathi website" or "Hindi website".** Not built yet.
- **Do not use the ₹99 Basic plan in public material.** It exists in the system
  but is switched off right now (`active = 0`). If you want a ₹99 entry price for
  the launch, turn it back on in Admin → Plans first, then update this file.
- **Do not invent customer counts or ratings.** The landing page says "500+
  businesses" and "4.9 rating" as placeholder design copy. Those are not real
  numbers yet, and a customer who checks will never come back.

## How to use this kit in week one

Monday — read `HOW-TO-USE-WEBSETU.md` yourself, end to end, doing each step.
You will find something confusing. Fix that first.

Tuesday — film video 1 and video 2 from `video-scripts.md`. Phone camera. No edits.

Wednesday — put the poster on WhatsApp status. Send the forward message from
`social-content.md` to 20 people who know shop owners.

Thursday and Friday — walk into 5 shops a day with the pitch from
`social-content.md`. Build the site in front of them. That is the whole demo.

The goal of week one is not sales. It is ten conversations with real shop
owners, and finding out which sentence makes them lean in.
