     5→# Build a Production-Ready Multi-Tenant Business Website SaaS Platform
     6→
     7→## 1. Product Vision
     8→
     9→Build a complete **multi-tenant SaaS platform** that allows any offline/local business that does not currently have a website to create and launch a professional business website using our platform.
    10→
    11→The platform should work like:
    12→
    13→**Customer visits our main website → selects “Create Your Website” → enters business details → selects a plan/template → adds business information, services, products, images, contact details, GMB/Google Business Profile link, social links, etc. → previews website → purchases plan → website goes LIVE automatically.**
    14→
    15→The system must be designed as a **real commercial SaaS product**, not as a simple website generator.
    16→
    17→The goal is to generate recurring revenue through:
    18→
    19→* Monthly subscriptions

   109→# 3. Important Multi-Tenant Requirement
   110→
   111→Every business must be completely isolated.
   112→
   113→Example:
   114→
   115→Business A:
   116→
   117→`business-a.OURDOMAIN.com`
   118→
   119→Business B:
   120→
   121→`business-b.OURDOMAIN.com`
   122→
   123→Business C:
   124→
   125→`business-c.OURDOMAIN.com`
   126→
   127→Each website must load only that business's data.
   128→
   129→Use tenant/business ID throughout:
   130→
   131→* Database
   132→* API
   133→* Authentication
   134→* Authorization
   135→* Storage
   136→* Website rendering
   137→* Analytics
   138→* Leads
   139→* SEO
   140→
   141→Never allow one tenant to access another tenant's data.
   142→
   143→Implement proper RBAC and tenant-level authorization.
