# Build a Production-Ready Multi-Tenant Business Website SaaS Platform

## 1. Product Vision

Build a complete **multi-tenant SaaS platform** that allows any offline/local business that does not currently have a website to create and launch a professional business website using our platform.

The platform should work like:

**Customer visits our main website → selects “Create Your Website” → enters business details → selects a plan/template → adds business information, services, products, images, contact details, GMB/Google Business Profile link, social links, etc. → previews website → purchases plan → website goes LIVE automatically.**

The system must be designed as a **real commercial SaaS product**, not as a simple website generator.

The goal is to generate recurring revenue through:

* Monthly subscriptions
* Yearly subscriptions
* Premium templates
* Premium SEO/AEO/GEO features
* Custom domain
* Business email
* Additional pages
* Additional locations
* Lead management
* Analytics
* AI content generation
* Maintenance packages
* Featured listings
* Add-on services

# 3. Important Multi-Tenant Requirement

Every business must be completely isolated.

Example:

Business A:

`business-a.OURDOMAIN.com`

Business B:

`business-b.OURDOMAIN.com`

Business C:

`business-c.OURDOMAIN.com`

Each website must load only that business's data.

Use tenant/business ID throughout:

* Database
* API
* Authentication
* Authorization
* Storage
* Website rendering
* Analytics
* Leads
* SEO

Never allow one tenant to access another tenant's data.

Implement proper RBAC and tenant-level authorization.
